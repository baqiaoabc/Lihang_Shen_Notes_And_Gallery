AI Acknowledgement

I used generative AI tools, including OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex, during the development of this Final Project. The publisher of these tools is OpenAI, and the tools were accessed through OpenAI services such as ChatGPT/Codex. The exact model version used was not always visible to me.

My use of AI was mainly to help organise the structure of my program, improve the clarity of comments and explanations, and assist with debugging some difficult issues that I encountered while developing the game. I also used AI to help improve some user-facing text, such as the beginner guide, menu descriptions, and gameplay instructions, so that the game would be clearer and more accessible for new players.

The core idea, game concept, and main design decisions were my own. AI was not used to decide the overall project topic or replace my own understanding of the programming concepts. Instead, I used AI as a support tool to help me think through how to structure the program more clearly, including whether it was appropriate to separate the game into different classes such as Paddle, Puck, Buff, Obstacle, and the main Game controller.

Some parts of the game logic were informed by my previous programming experience. For example, the puck movement and bounce behaviour were partly based on logic I had previously used in a COMP9003 bouncing ball game, especially updating object positions each frame and changing direction after collisions. I adapted this logic for the air hockey context, including paddle collisions, wall collisions, obstacle collisions, goals, different puck speeds, and game state changes.

Air hockey is a common game topic, so some general ideas in my project are naturally similar to other air hockey or bouncing ball games, such as having paddles, goals, a puck, wall collisions, and rebound behaviour. However, I did not copy a specific existing air hockey project. I built my version based on my own understanding of the game mechanics and extended the basic air hockey idea with my own design choices. For example, I added different map modes, obstacle layouts, a buff system, adjustable puck speed, configurable match time, buff spawn modes, a beginner guide, and sudden-death overtime. These features were designed and adapted for my own project rather than copied from another source.

I also used AI to help identify and fix bugs, especially when the menu, beginner guide screen, buff display, collision behaviour, and game states did not behave as expected. Any AI suggestions were reviewed, tested, and modified by me before being included in the final project. I did not rely on AI blindly; I checked the code, ran the program, and made sure I understood the changes.

I have tried to be transparent about my use of AI and other resources. I reviewed and tested all AI-assisted suggestions before including them, and I understand that I am responsible for the originality, correctness, and integrity of the submitted work.

All final design choices, testing, debugging decisions, and submitted code are my own responsibility. I understand that my use of AI must comply with the course and University policies, and I accept responsibility for any assessment consequences if my use is found to be inappropriate.
